# Kaya design direction

Contemporary Korean concept-store restraint with a recognisable pink accent.
Scene: a Colombian customer browsing Korean skincare and music on a phone in daylight.
Preserve pink #FF6B9D and mint #B8E6D9; use clear light surfaces and ink text.
Use Korean place photography and honest product photography, not exotic stereotypes.
No invented Hangul translation, floating pills, decorative blobs or false reviews.
Type has zero letter spacing, fixed responsive steps and readable hierarchy.
Use full-width sections, a photographic hero and a small product grid.
Buttons have clear actions and Lucide icons. Support keyboard and reduced motion.
Administrative UI uses compact rows and forms, not marketing composition.
